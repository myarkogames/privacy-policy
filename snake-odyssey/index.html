Snake Odyssey 3D: Eat It All: Privacy Policy

Developer: Myarko Games · App ID: com.myarkogames.snakeodyssey · Last updated: 2026-10-28

This policy explains how the mobile game Snake Odyssey 3D: Eat It All ("the app"), published by Myarko Games on Google Play, handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any account.

Information stored on your device

Game progress and settings (levels, coins, unlocked skins, sound options) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

The app relies on these services, which may process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play: App distribution and updates. Google may collect install, device and crash diagnostics (Android vitals) under its own policy. Privacy policy.

Advertising and purchases

The app does not show third-party ads and does not use your advertising ID.

The app has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and data deletion

Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete. You can remove all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For data held by the services above, use their own tools linked in their policies.

Security

The app does not transmit your game data over the network, so it stays protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change, for example when a new service is added, and change the "Last updated" date above.
